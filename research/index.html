Research

Technology, labour demand and the selectivity of migration

Dissertation

Shifting Grounds: Technological Transformation, Labour Demand, and the Selectivity of Global Migration.

My dissertation examines how differences in technological development influence where people move, and which skills are in demand.

Research questions

  1. Technological change.How do AI, automation and digitalisation reshape economic opportunity?
  2. Labour demand.Which skills are complemented or displaced?
  3. Migration.How does technological change affect international mobility?

Methods: panel data, gravity models and applied econometrics.
