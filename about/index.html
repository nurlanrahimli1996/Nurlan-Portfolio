SHORT BIOGRAPHY

Nurlan Rahimli

I am a doctoral researcher in Economics at Kozminski University in Warsaw. My research interests lie at the intersection of technological transformation, labour demand and international migration. I am especially interested in the geography of economic opportunity, the distributional consequences of technological change, and the decisions people make about mobility.

Before and alongside my doctoral studies, I built professional experience in data analytics in international organizations, bringing together empirical curiosity and applied problem-solving.

Beyond formal research, I write about ideas, society, travel and personal questions in Free.
