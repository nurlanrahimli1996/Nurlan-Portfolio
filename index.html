Shifting Grounds.

Technological Transformation, Labour Demand, and the Selectivity of Global Migration

The project investigates whether technological differences between countries influence the scale and skill composition of cross-border migration, linking technological adoption to labour demand and migration incentives.

APPROACHGravity models · Panel data · Skill heterogeneity

05 / ABOUT

Research at the intersection of people, markets, and technology.

I am Nurlan Rahimli, a doctoral researcher in Economics at Kozminski University in Warsaw. My interests connect international migration, labour economics, technological transformation, and empirical research.

Alongside academic work, my experience in data analysis informs a practical approach to making evidence accessible—through clear methods, thoughtful visualizations, and careful interpretation.
